Two things go wrong when buying paint. Buying too little means a second trip, and a risk that the new tin is a slightly different batch. Buying too much means leftover paint that will sit in a cupboard for six years and then go to a tip.

The arithmetic is not hard. The part people get wrong is the adjustments.

## The base calculation

**Wall area** = perimeter × height.

For a room 4 m × 3 m with 2.4 m ceilings: perimeter is (4 + 3) × 2 = 14 m, so wall area is 14 × 2.4 = **33.6 m²**.

**Subtract openings.** A standard door is about 1.8 m², a typical window 1.5–2 m². With one door and one window, subtract roughly 3.5 m², leaving about **30 m²**.

**Multiply by coats.** Two coats is the realistic default, so **60 m²** of coverage needed.

**Divide by coverage.** At a typical 12 m² per litre, that is **5 litres** — so a 5 L tin, with the understanding that it will be close.

## Why the tin's coverage figure is optimistic

Manufacturers quote coverage under favourable conditions: a smooth, sealed, previously painted surface, applied by roller at the intended thickness.

Real coverage is often 20–40% lower. The main causes:

**Porous surfaces.** New plaster, bare filler and old chalky emulsion absorb paint. New plaster in particular can take nearly twice as much on the first coat, which is why a thinned mist coat is standard practice — it seals the surface far more cheaply than full-strength paint does.

**Texture.** Woodchip, textured plaster or heavy brush marks increase actual surface area substantially. Bare brick or blockwork can halve coverage.

**Colour changes.** Painting light over dark, or over a strong colour, may need three coats. Reds, deep yellows and some oranges have weak opacity and often need three even over white — a known property of the pigments, not a defect.

**Application method.** Spraying loses paint to overspray. A roller with too long a nap holds more paint and lays it on thicker.

Typical real-world coverage, per litre:

| Surface | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Previously painted, smooth | 12–14 m² |
| New plaster (mist coat) | 6–8 m² |
| New plaster (subsequent coats) | 10–12 m² |
| Textured / woodchip | 8–10 m² |
| Bare brick or block | 5–7 m² |
| Gloss on wood (per coat) | 14–16 m² |

## Ceilings, and the thing people forget

Ceiling area is simply length × width — 12 m² for the room above. At two coats that is 24 m², or 2 litres.

Ceilings are usually painted with a dedicated matt emulsion, so this is a separate tin, not part of the wall figure. It is the most commonly forgotten item on a paint list.

## Woodwork

Skirting, architrave, doors and window frames are estimated by length rather than area.

A rough rule: skirting and architrave consume about 1 litre per 60–70 m of run, per coat. A door needs roughly 0.25 litres per coat per side, including the frame.

Woodwork almost always needs primer or undercoat on bare wood, plus two topcoats. Budget three coats total.

## Buying it

**Round up to tin sizes, not to litres.** Paint is sold in 1, 2.5, 5 and 10 L tins. If you need 6 litres, a 5 L plus a 2.5 L costs less than two 5 L tins and wastes less.

**Check price per litre, not per tin.** Larger tins are usually cheaper per litre, but not always, and promotions distort this constantly. The [unit price comparator](/unit-price-comparator/) settles it in a few seconds.

**Buy all of a colour at once, and check batch numbers match.** Mixed-to-order colours can vary slightly between batches. If you must use two batches, decant them into a larger container and mix — "boxing" — so any variation is uniform rather than appearing as a line down a wall.

**Keep about half a litre** for touch-ups, in a small sealed container rather than a part-empty tin. A tin with more air than paint skins over within a year.

## A worked example

A 4 × 3 m bedroom, 2.4 m ceilings, one door, one window, previously painted walls in a similar shade, plus ceiling and skirting:

| Item | Area | Coats | Paint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walls | 30 m² | 2 | 5 L |
| Ceiling | 12 m² | 2 | 2.5 L |
| Skirting (14 m) | — | 2 | 0.5 L |
| **Buy** | | | **5 L walls, 2.5 L ceiling, 1 L gloss** |

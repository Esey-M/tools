Contact

Corrections and tool requests are both genuinely welcome.

Reporting an error

This is the most useful message you can send. Please include:

  • Which tool you were using
  • What you entered — the exact numbers or text
  • What it produced, and what you expected instead

Arithmetic bugs get fixed quickly, and the page's "last reviewed" date is updated when they are.

Requesting a tool

If something you need is missing, say what the task is rather than what the tool should be called. Knowing that you are trying to work out how much paint to buy for a room with two windows is more useful than a request for a "paint calculator", because it tells us what the inputs need to be.

Getting in touch

Email: hello@cinchpad.com

We read everything. Replies to genuine questions usually arrive within a few days.

What we cannot help with

We cannot give medical, financial, legal or tax advice, or interpret what a particular result means for you personally. The tools do arithmetic; a professional who knows your situation does the rest.

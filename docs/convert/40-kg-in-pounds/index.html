How the conversion works

One pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, so dividing by that figure converts kilograms to pounds precisely.

40 ÷ 0.45359237 = 88.1849 pounds

For mental arithmetic, doubling the kilograms and adding 10% gets you close: 40 × 2 = 80, plus 10% is 88 — against an exact 88.2.

Britain and Ireland usually express body weight in stones, where one stone is 14 pounds. 88.2 ÷ 14 gives 6 stone 4.2 pounds.

What is 40 kg in stone?

Is the conversion exact?

Yes. Since 1959 the international pound has been defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, so the factor is a definition rather than a measurement.

What is the quick way to do this in my head?
